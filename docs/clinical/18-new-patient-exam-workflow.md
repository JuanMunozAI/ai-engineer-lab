# New Patient Exam Workflow (Clinical Side)

1. Hygienist reviews medical history and takes blood pressure. Blood pressure above **180/110** means no treatment today; refer to their physician.
2. X-rays: full-mouth series (D0210) or panoramic plus bitewings, unless the patient brings X-rays less than 12 months old.
3. Intraoral photos.
4. Periodontal charting (pocket depths at 6 points per tooth).
5. Doctor completes the comprehensive exam (D0150) and reviews findings with the patient.
6. Hygienist completes cleaning (D1110) if pockets are mostly **3 mm or less**. Pockets of **4 mm or more** with bleeding and bone loss: schedule scaling and root planing instead.
7. Treatment coordinator presents the treatment plan and cost estimate before the patient leaves.
