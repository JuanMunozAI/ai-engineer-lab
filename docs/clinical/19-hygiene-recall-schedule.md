# Hygiene Recall Schedule

- Healthy adults: every **6 months** (cleaning D1110 and periodic exam D0120)
- Patients who had scaling and root planing: periodontal maintenance (D4910) every **3 months** for at least the first year
- Children: every 6 months (D1120), with fluoride varnish (D1206)
- High cavity risk patients: every 3 to 4 months with fluoride

Book the next recall appointment **before the patient leaves**. Patients without a booked recall go on the "Unscheduled Recall" list, which the front desk calls every Wednesday.

Check the patient's insurance frequency rule before booking. Some plans count per calendar year, others need 6 months between visits.
