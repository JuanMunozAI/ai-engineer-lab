# X-Ray Guidelines (Office Policy)

The doctor makes the final decision for each patient. Office defaults:

| X-ray | Adults | Children |
|---|---|---|
| Bitewings (D0272/D0274) | Every 12 months; every 24 months for low-risk adults | Every 6 to 12 months if cavity risk is high |
| Full-mouth series (D0210) | Every 3 to 5 years | Rarely; doctor decides |
| Panoramic (D0330) | New patients, wisdom tooth evaluation, or every 5 years | Around age 7 and age 15 for growth evaluation |

- Always use the lead apron with thyroid collar.
- Ask women of childbearing age if they might be pregnant. If yes, X-rays are taken only for emergencies.
- Check insurance frequency limits before taking X-rays; if the plan will not pay, tell the patient the self-pay cost first ($65 for bitewings, $145 for full-mouth series, $125 for panoramic).
