# Crown Appointment Workflow

## Visit 1: Preparation (90 minutes)
1. Confirm pre-authorization status and the patient's estimated cost.
2. Numb the area, remove decay, and do a core buildup (D2950) if not enough tooth remains.
3. Shape the tooth and take a digital scan.
4. Place a temporary crown.
5. Send the scan to the lab (Coastline Dental Lab, fictional). Standard turnaround: **10 business days**.

## Visit 2: Seat (45 minutes)
Book the seat appointment **2 weeks** after the prep. Confirm the crown has arrived from the lab 2 days before.

## Billing
Bill the crown (D2740) on the **seat date**, not the prep date. Insurance pays on the date the crown is permanently placed.

## Temporary crown came off
Patient should call the office. If after hours, they can put it back with denture adhesive or toothpaste and come in the next business day. It is not an emergency unless there is severe pain.
