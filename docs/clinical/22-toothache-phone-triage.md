# Toothache Phone Triage (Front Desk Script)

Ask the caller:
1. Which tooth or area hurts, and since when?
2. On a scale of 1 to 10, how bad is the pain?
3. Is there any swelling? Where?
4. Do you have a fever?
5. Does the pain wake you up at night or last a long time after hot or cold?

## What to do
- Swelling affecting breathing or swallowing, or fever with facial swelling: tell them to go to the ER now.
- Pain 7 or higher, swelling, or pain that wakes them at night: book the next emergency slot today.
- Pain under 7 with no swelling: book within 2 business days.
- Sensitivity only to cold that goes away quickly: book a regular exam and suggest sensitivity toothpaste.

Never diagnose or recommend prescription medicine over the phone. Only the doctor can do that.
