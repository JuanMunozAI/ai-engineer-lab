# Front Desk FAQ (Internal)

**A patient wants to book a cleaning but their last one was 5 months ago. Can we?**
Check their plan. SunCoast PPO counts 2 per calendar year, so it may be fine in a new year. BlueWave HMO and SunState Kids need 6 months between cleanings. If it will not be covered, tell the patient the self-pay fee ($120 adult cleaning) before booking.

**A patient asks how much a crown costs.**
Self-pay is $1,250 plus $295 if a buildup is needed. With insurance, offer a pre-authorization; never guess.

**Someone calls asking if their spouse has an appointment today.**
Do not confirm or deny unless the spouse is listed as an authorized contact in the chart.

**A patient wants to pay half now and half later.**
Offer the in-house plan if treatment is over $500 (25% down, up to 6 months).

**The card reader is down.**
Use the backup reader in the office manager's top drawer, or take the payment through the ChairSide PM online payment link.

**Who approves fee waivers?**
Front desk can waive one late fee per patient per year. Anything else goes to Carla Simmons.
