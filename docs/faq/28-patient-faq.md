# Patient FAQ

## Do you take my insurance?
We are in network with SunCoast Dental PPO, BlueWave Dental HMO and the SunState Kids program. We see patients with other plans as out of network.

## How often should I get a cleaning?
Every 6 months for most people. Patients treated for gum disease usually need maintenance every 3 months.

## Do you offer payment plans?
Yes, for treatment over $500. Ask about the Lakeside Smile Club if you do not have insurance.

## Do you see children?
Yes, starting by age 1. A parent or guardian must come to the first visit.

## What if I need to cancel?
Please give us 48 hours' notice to avoid a fee.

## Is parking available?
Free parking in the lot behind the building. Accessible parking is next to the main entrance.

## Do you offer sedation?
We offer nitrous oxide (laughing gas) for $55 per visit. For IV sedation we refer to a partner oral surgeon.
