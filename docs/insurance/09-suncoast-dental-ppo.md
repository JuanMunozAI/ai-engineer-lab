# SunCoast Dental PPO (fictional plan): Plan Summary

Lakeside Family Dental is **in network** with SunCoast Dental PPO.

| Item | Coverage |
|---|---|
| Annual maximum | $1,500 per person |
| Deductible | $50 per person, $150 per family; waived for preventive |
| Preventive (exams, cleanings, X-rays) | 100% |
| Basic (fillings, simple extractions) | 80% after deductible |
| Major (crowns, bridges, dentures) | 50% after deductible |
| Waiting period for major | 12 months for new members |

## Frequency limits
- Exams and cleanings: **2 per calendar year** (not every 6 months)
- Bitewing X-rays: 1 set per calendar year
- Full-mouth or panoramic X-ray: 1 every **5 years**
- Fluoride: covered under age **16**, twice per year
- Sealants: permanent molars, under age **14**, once per tooth per lifetime
- Crowns: replacement once every **5 years** per tooth

## Other rules
- Pre-authorization required for any single treatment over **$400**.
- Periodontal maintenance (D4910) counts toward the 2 cleanings per year.
- Claims must be filed within **90 days** of the date of service.
- Payer ID for electronic claims: SCD01.
