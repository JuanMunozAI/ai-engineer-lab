# BlueWave Dental HMO (fictional plan): Plan Summary

BlueWave is an HMO plan. Patients must choose Lakeside Family Dental as their assigned office before their visit, or the plan will not pay.

- No annual maximum and no deductible.
- Patients pay a fixed **copay** per procedure instead of a percentage.

| Procedure | Patient copay |
|---|---|
| Exam, cleaning, X-rays | $0 |
| Emergency exam (D0140) | $15 |
| One-surface composite filling (D2391) | $45 |
| Porcelain crown (D2740) | $450 |
| Scaling and root planing per quadrant (D4341) | $70 |

## Frequency limits
- Cleanings: 2 per year, at least **6 months apart** (unlike SunCoast PPO, which only counts calendar year)
- Full-mouth X-rays: 1 every 3 years

## Referrals
Specialist care (oral surgeon, endodontist) requires a referral approved by BlueWave **before** the appointment. Approval takes 5 to 7 business days.

## Assignment check
Before every visit, confirm on the BlueWave portal that the patient is still assigned to our office. Assignments can change monthly.
