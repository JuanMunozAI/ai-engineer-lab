# GuardStar Dental PPO (fictional plan): Plan Summary

Lakeside Family Dental is **out of network** with GuardStar. Patients may still be seen; see Out-of-Network Billing.

| Item | Coverage (out of network) |
|---|---|
| Annual maximum | $1,000 per person |
| Deductible | $75 per person |
| Preventive | 80% of GuardStar's allowed amount |
| Basic | 60% after deductible |
| Major | 40% after deductible |

## Frequency limits
- Cleanings: 2 per calendar year
- Bitewing X-rays: 1 set every **2 years** for adults
- Crowns: once every **7 years** per tooth

## Notes
- GuardStar pays the **patient** directly for out-of-network claims, not the office. Collect the full fee at the time of service and give the patient a claim form.
- No waiting periods.
