# Insurance Verification Procedure

Verify insurance for every patient **3 business days** before their appointment, and again on the day of the visit if the appointment was booked within 3 days.

## Steps
1. Open the patient's insurance tab in ChairSide PM.
2. Log in to the insurance company's provider portal (portal logins are in the shared password manager, folder "Insurance Portals").
3. Confirm: plan is active, subscriber name and date of birth, group number, remaining annual maximum, deductible met, and frequency history (last cleaning date, last X-ray dates).
4. Record the verification in ChairSide PM using the "Ins Verified" note template, including the representative's name and reference number if you called.
5. If coverage is **inactive**, call the patient the same day and explain they will be self-pay unless they provide new insurance.

## Red flags to tell the patient before the visit
- Annual maximum is used up or nearly used up
- Last cleaning was too recent for the plan's frequency rule
- HMO patient is not assigned to our office
