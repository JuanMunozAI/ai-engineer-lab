# Claim Submission Procedure

## Timing
Submit claims electronically **the same day** as the appointment. Every insurer has a filing deadline; SunCoast PPO is 90 days.

## Required attachments
| Procedure | Attachment needed |
|---|---|
| Crown (D2740) | Pre-op X-ray of the tooth and a narrative of why the crown is needed |
| Core buildup (D2950) | Pre-op X-ray |
| Scaling and root planing (D4341, D4342) | Full periodontal charting (pocket depths) and X-rays from the last 12 months |
| Extraction | Pre-op X-ray |

## Required fields
Every claim must include: tooth number (for tooth-specific codes), surfaces (for fillings), quadrant (for D4341/D4342), the rendering provider's NPI, and the date of service.

## Common rejection reasons
1. Missing tooth number or surface
2. Missing X-ray attachment
3. Frequency limit exceeded
4. Patient not eligible on date of service
5. Wrong rendering provider

## Follow-up
Check the ChairSide PM outstanding claims report every **Monday**. Any claim unpaid after **30 days** gets a call to the insurer.
