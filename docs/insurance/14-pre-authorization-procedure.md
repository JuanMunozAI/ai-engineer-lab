# Pre-Authorization (Pre-Treatment Estimate) Procedure

A pre-authorization asks the insurer to confirm what it will pay before treatment starts.

## When it is required
- SunCoast PPO: any single treatment over $400
- BlueWave HMO: all specialist referrals
- Our office policy: any treatment plan over **$1,000** for any insurance, even if the insurer does not require it, so the patient knows their cost

## Steps
1. Doctor finalizes the treatment plan in ChairSide PM.
2. Submit the pre-authorization electronically with the same attachments a claim would need.
3. Tell the patient most insurers respond within **2 to 4 weeks**.
4. When the response arrives, call the patient to review their estimated cost and schedule.

Urgent treatment (infection, severe pain) does not wait for a pre-authorization. The doctor documents the urgency in the chart.
