# Out-of-Network Billing

We are out of network with GuardStar Dental PPO and any plan not listed as in network in ChairSide PM.

- Out-of-network patients pay our **full office fee** at the time of service.
- As a courtesy, we file the claim for them, but the insurer may pay the patient directly.
- Tell patients before treatment that their insurer may pay less than our fee, and they are responsible for the difference ("balance billing").
- Provide a printed fee estimate for any treatment over $300.
