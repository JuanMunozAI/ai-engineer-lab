# SunState Kids Dental Program (fictional state program)

SunState Kids is a fictional state-funded dental program for children from qualifying families. Lakeside Family Dental accepts it for patients **under 21**.

- No copays or deductibles for covered services.
- Covered: exams, cleanings, X-rays, fluoride, sealants, fillings, simple extractions.
- Not covered: cosmetic treatment, porcelain crowns on back teeth (stainless steel crowns are covered instead).
- Cleanings: 2 per year, at least **6 months apart**.
- Eligibility must be checked **on the day of the visit**, because coverage can end any month.
- Patients cannot be charged a no-show fee under this program.
