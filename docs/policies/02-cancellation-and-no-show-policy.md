# Cancellation and No-Show Policy

## Notice required
Patients must cancel or reschedule at least **48 hours** before their appointment.

## Fees
- Late cancellation (less than 48 hours): **$35** for hygiene appointments, **$50** for doctor appointments.
- No-show (patient does not arrive and does not call): **$75**.
- Fees are waived once per patient per calendar year at the front desk's discretion. Note the waiver in the patient's account notes.
- Medical emergencies and severe weather (county-declared) are always waived.

## Three-strike rule
After **three** no-shows within 12 months, the patient may only book same-day appointments for the next 6 months. The office manager must approve any exception.

## Children
For patients under 18, the fee is charged to the responsible party on the account.

## Confirmations
Automated text confirmations go out 72 hours and 24 hours before the appointment. A missed confirmation does not excuse a no-show.
