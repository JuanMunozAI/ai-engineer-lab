# New Patient Intake Procedure

## Before the first visit
1. Send the digital intake packet link by text as soon as the appointment is booked.
2. The packet includes: medical history, dental history, HIPAA acknowledgment, financial policy, and insurance information.
3. Ask the patient to upload a photo of the front and back of their insurance card and a photo ID.
4. Run insurance verification at least **3 business days** before the visit (see Insurance Verification Procedure).

## Day of the visit
- New patients should arrive **15 minutes early**.
- If the packet is not complete, give the patient a tablet in the waiting room.
- Scan the photo ID and insurance card if not uploaded.

## Standard new patient appointment
- Length: 90 minutes with a hygienist and doctor.
- Typical codes: comprehensive exam (D0150), full-mouth X-rays (D0210) or panoramic (D0330), and adult cleaning (D1110) if the patient has no active gum disease.
- If the doctor diagnoses periodontal disease, the cleaning is rescheduled as scaling and root planing (D4341 or D4342).

## Medical alerts
Flag in the chart any patient who reports: heart valve replacement, joint replacement within the last 2 years, blood thinners, bisphosphonate use, or latex allergy. The doctor must review flagged charts before treatment begins.
