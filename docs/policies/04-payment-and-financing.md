# Payment and Financing Policy

## When payment is due
Patient portions are due at the time of service. We collect the **estimated** patient portion based on insurance verification; any difference is billed after the insurance pays.

## Accepted payment
Cash, checks, all major credit and debit cards, HSA and FSA cards. We do not accept checks for balances over $1,000.

## Financing
- In-house payment plans are available for treatment over **$500**.
- Plans require 25% down and the balance split over a maximum of **6 months**, interest free.
- Treatment over $3,000 is referred to our third-party financing partner, BrightPay Health (fictional), which offers 12 to 24 month plans.

## Discounts
- 5% off for paying the full treatment plan in advance (not combined with insurance).
- Lakeside Smile Club (in-house membership for patients without insurance): $29 per month or $299 per year. Covers 2 cleanings, 2 exams, 1 set of bitewing X-rays per year, and 20% off other treatment.

## Collections
Balances over 90 days are sent a final notice. Balances over 120 days may be sent to collections after the office manager approves.
