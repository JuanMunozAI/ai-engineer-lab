# Patient Records and Privacy Procedures

All staff complete privacy training on hire and every January.

## Records requests
- Patients may request a copy of their records using the Records Release Form.
- Requests are fulfilled within **15 business days**.
- Electronic copies (secure email or patient portal) are free. Paper copies are $0.50 per page after the first 10 pages.
- X-rays are provided digitally on request.

## Releasing records to another office
Requires a signed release from the patient (or parent/guardian for minors). Never fax or email records without the signed form on file.

## Phone calls
- Verify the caller's identity with full name and date of birth before discussing any appointment, balance, or treatment.
- Do not leave treatment details in voicemails. Leave only: name of the office, a callback number, and "regarding your appointment."

## Workstations
Lock your screen (Windows key + L) whenever you leave the desk. Never write passwords on sticky notes.

## Reporting
Report any suspected privacy issue to the office manager, Carla Simmons, the same day.
