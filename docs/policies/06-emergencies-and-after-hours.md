# Dental Emergencies and After-Hours Care

## What counts as a dental emergency
- Severe tooth pain not controlled by over-the-counter medicine
- Swelling of the face or jaw
- A knocked-out permanent tooth
- Bleeding that does not stop after 20 minutes of firm pressure
- A broken tooth with sharp edges or exposed nerve

## During office hours
Emergency patients of record are seen the **same day**. Two emergency slots are held each day: 10:30 AM and 2:30 PM (Friday: 10:30 AM only). New patients with emergencies are seen if a slot is open; the emergency exam fee is $95 (code D0140).

## After hours
The after-hours voicemail gives the on-call doctor's pager number: (407) 555-0177. The on-call doctor rotates weekly between Dr. Ruiz and Dr. Bell.

## When to send to the ER
Tell the patient to go to the emergency room or call 911 if they have swelling that affects breathing or swallowing, a high fever with facial swelling, or a jaw injury from an accident.

## Knocked-out tooth instructions
Pick it up by the crown (not the root), rinse gently, try to put it back in the socket or keep it in milk, and come in within **60 minutes**.
