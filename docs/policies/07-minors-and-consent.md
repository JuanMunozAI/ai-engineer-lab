# Treating Minors and Consent

- Patients under **18** need a parent or legal guardian to sign consent forms.
- A parent or guardian must be present for the **first visit** and for any treatment involving numbing, extractions, or sedation.
- For routine cleanings after the first visit, a minor aged **14 to 17** may attend alone if a signed Minor Attendance Form is on file.
- Grandparents or other adults may bring a child only if the parent has signed an Authorized Adult Form naming that person.
- Divorced or separated parents: either parent with legal custody may consent unless a court order on file says otherwise. Upload any court order to the chart.

## First dental visit
We recommend a first visit by the child's first birthday or within 6 months of the first tooth.
