# Late Arrival Policy

- Patients who arrive **up to 10 minutes late** are seen, but the appointment may be shortened.
- Patients who arrive **more than 15 minutes late** for a hygiene visit, or **more than 20 minutes late** for a doctor visit, must be rescheduled unless the provider agrees to see them.
- A late arrival that has to be rescheduled counts as a late cancellation and the late cancellation fee applies (see Cancellation and No-Show Policy).
- Call patients who are 5 minutes late to check on them.
