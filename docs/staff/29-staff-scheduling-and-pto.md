# Staff Scheduling and Time Off

- Schedules are posted every other Friday for the following two weeks.
- Request paid time off (PTO) at least **3 weeks** in advance through the staff portal.
- No more than **one hygienist** and **one front desk** person may be off on the same day.
- PTO accrual: 1 hour per 25 hours worked, up to 80 hours per year. Up to 40 unused hours roll over.
- Call-outs: call (not text) the office manager at least **2 hours** before your shift.
- The office closes early on the last Friday of each month at 11:00 AM for the staff meeting.
- Continuing education: the office pays up to $500 per year per clinical staff member for approved courses.
