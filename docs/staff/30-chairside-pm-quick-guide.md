# ChairSide PM Quick Guide (fictional practice software)

## Daily tasks
- **Morning:** run the Day Sheet report, confirm all of today's patients are verified, and print the route slips.
- **End of day:** run the Daily Deposit report, balance it against the card reader total, and lock the cash drawer.

## Where things are
- Insurance verification notes: patient > Insurance tab > Notes
- Treatment plans: patient > Tx Plan tab
- Outstanding claims: Reports > Insurance > Outstanding Claims (run every Monday)
- Unscheduled recall list: Reports > Recall > Unscheduled (call every Wednesday)

## Common fixes
- "Claim rejected: missing tooth number" — open the procedure, add the tooth number, and resubmit from Claims > Rejected.
- A patient shows a duplicate chart — do not merge yourself. Send both chart numbers to the office manager.
